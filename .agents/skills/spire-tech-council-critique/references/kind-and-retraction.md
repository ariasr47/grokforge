# Kind and retraction

## technical vs product

| kind | Settled by | Examples |
| --- | --- | --- |
| technical | Roles / existing mechanism | Storage already supports a tenant setting; field types; feasibility |
| product | Human Decision card | Retention days as product choice; scope expansions; priority tradeoffs |

Mis-tagging product as technical hides a Decision card. Mis-tagging technical as product burns the
operator on something the council should resolve.

## Retraction as success

If R1 was wrong in light of peer positions, put the correction in `agree` or a conflict that quotes
yourself when appropriate — honesty beats defending a dead claim. Mechanical self-revision support
in the pipeline treats retraction as a win, not a failure.
