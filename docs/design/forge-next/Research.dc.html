Research & redesign brief · 1 September 2026 · proposal for the operator

Forge, next: the same house, built properly

Voidglass stays. Geist stays. Review, Trusted and Bypass stay. What changes is execution: one palette instead of two, one box per idea instead of seven, a thread you can trust at a glance, and an approval surface that respects the reader's attention. This brief records what we have, what the field has converged on, and what the canvas beside it proposes.

Decision · 1 September 2026 · Direction A chosen and polished; B and C moved to the "Not chosen" page.

1 · What we have today

Audit of apps/shell at 0.6.6, the dogfood screenshots, and the design canon in .spire/clusters/tech/context/DESIGN_SYSTEM.md.

  • Two palettes in one theme. Tokens declare cyan #5ce1ff and violet #a78bfa (Voidglass, "locked default"), but most of chrome.css, run.css, dock.css and settings.css hardcode the Aeon mint rgba(125,255,232) and lilac rgba(196,181,253). The shipped app is mint-teal while the canon is cyan; newer chips use cyan, so both are on screen at once. docs/brand/README.md names Aeon as primary; tokens.css names Voidglass. This disagreement is the root cause.
  • Boxes inside boxes. One Code turn renders up to nine bordered containers: a sticky "You" card, four provenance chips, Thought, Earlier / Mid-turn, Tool activity, File changes, the answer card, an "Answered" strip and a "Your turn" delimiter. Every element has the same weight, so nothing leads. docs/dogfood/edit-turn.png, forge-file-changes.png
  • Chrome before content. Dev ribbon, a nine-item topbar (brand, mode word, segmented control, breadcrumb, three status pills, Ctrl+K, Settings) and a sidebar with a label pill, gradient CTA, search, a workspace card holding a session card, a path row, Messages / Settings tabs and a help line. The sidebar is also double-padded (aside padding plus every child's own).
  • The composer is four rows tall. A pill textarea with three external buttons, an Effort row of four pills, Plan, project-instructions chip, agent chip, meta, policy and a hint line. A static --composer-height: 160px positions the dock and toasts, yet the real height varies with wrapping.
  • Motion behind reading surfaces. A canvas paints up to 72 twinkling stars and two nebulae behind the transcript. Particles under 14px text cost contrast and read as generic "AI product".
  • Copy that performs instead of informs. "Speak into the continuum…", "YOUR TURN — TYPE THE NEXT MESSAGE BELOW", every group titled "Tool activity", a "Failed" row whose body says "completed", "Answered" as a strip.
  • Tool rows. Mono 11px throughout, a "Peek path" button under every row, the group body scrolling inside the transcript (nested scroll). The 2000px capture shows the thread held to a 700px column with a floating "You" card top-right and 60% of the window empty. screen-overflow-cut-off-scroll.png
  • Component debt. Button has three variants and no sizes; eight ad-hoc !important size overrides; fourteen hand-styled pill variants; two transcript renderers (RunSurface and MessageList); a 90 KB PNG brand mark next to an unused SVG; light theme declared twice with conflicting values; --ok referenced but never defined.
  • Contrast fixes were needed at 1.27:1 to 4.15:1 during the last accessibility sweep, all from hardcoded colors bypassing tokens.

Worth keeping, and built on here

  • The house / guest split: Forge draws, the vendor decides when to think, tool, or ask.
  • Review · Trusted · Bypass, mapped onto the guest's permission prompts. This is the moat; the redesign makes it visible rather than hiding it in Settings.
  • Plan → File changes → Verify → Git review as the loop. The new Changes dock and Review surface are that loop in one place.
  • Activity groups instead of a card per call; the amber dock as attention; honest copy about limits ("not Forge workers", the unsigned installer); the Y · S · N · A · R keys.

3 · What has converged, and where Forge can win

Steer without stoppingCodex, Claude, Zed, Conductor and Warp all let you type while the agent runs and choose between steering now and queueing. Forge's composer gets Steer ⏎ · Queue ⇧⏎ · Stop esc.
Receipts, not logsClaude's Normal mode, Warp's zero-height blocks and Antigravity's artifacts agree: collapse tool calls into summaries, expand on demand. Forge's receipts are one verb-first line per call.
Plan review is where attention worksAnthropic's own numbers, from its August 2026 "Auto mode" post: people reject 39% of plans and 3% of permission prompts, and approve 97% of prompts reflexively. Forge keeps Review as default but makes the Plan gate the richest card, and offers "allow this class for the session" on every shell prompt.
Needs-you as a stateWarp's Blocked badge, Zed's notify-when-waiting, Claude's status filter. Forge's sidebar leads with a Needs-you group, the spine's last node turns amber, and the home screen opens on what needs you.
A visible context meterCodex users noticed the moment theirs shrank. Forge shows a labeled ring in the composer, always.
Per-hunk review with a way to talk backWarp, Conductor and Claude let you comment on a line and send it to the agent. Forge's Review surface pairs per-hunk accept with "Ask Grok to change this file".
Findability from day oneSearch, filter by state, and archive on finish, before there are fifty sessions, not after.
Where nobody is good yetA native-feeling Windows desktop; a clear "which session is running what" answer; a shell that is not an IDE and so never buries you under panes. That is Forge's lane.

4 · The redesign

Principles

  • Answer first. Hierarchy in the thread is fixed: the answer, then your prompt, then receipts, then metadata. Type size and contrast follow that order and nothing else.
  • One box per idea. A turn is a node on the spine, a prompt block, an optional thought line, one receipts group, prose. Strips, delimiters and status cards are gone; their information lives in the node and the composer state.
  • Amber means you. Only things that need a human are amber: the sidebar group, the node, the gate, the toast. Cyan is action and life; violet is thought and plans; mint and rose are verdicts.
  • Keys everywhere. Every gate shows its keys, palette rows list their shortcuts, and A and R still accept and reject hunks. ⏎ allows, S trusts for the session, esc denies; Y and N stay as aliases.
  • Motion explains. Ten interactions, each tied to a state change (see the components sheet). Nothing loops except live dots and one shimmer; the star field becomes a static aurora at the top edge and the stars move to the Calm-off preference.
  • Honest words. Sentence case, plain verbs, no theater: "Reply or steer Grok… @ file · / command", "Waiting for you", "exit 2 · 3 errors", "Rejecting restores the pre-run copy Forge kept".

System changes

PaletteOne: the Voidglass tokens. Retire every hardcoded rgba(125,255,232) and rgba(196,181,253). Add three surfaces: glass 4%, glass raised 7%, floating #0e1016 at 92% with 20px blur. RadiusComposer and floating layers 16; cards 12–14; controls 10; chips pill. amends canon 16 / 12 TypeProse 15 / 1.62 at a 640px measure; UI 13; labels 11 caps only for section names; mono 12 only for machine text. ButtonOne component, sizes 28 / 32 / 36, tones CTA · accent · default · ghost · danger. Removes the eight !important overrides. ChipOne component with quiet, default, on and amber tones replaces fourteen pill variants. Brand markThe SVG nucleus at 18 px in chrome, 44 px on Home. The PNG goes back to the installer only, as the brand doc already rules. ComposerOne row when idle, two when you type; in flow, no static height. Plan, effort and policy chips sit beside the field with the context ring. While Grok runs the same field steers: Steer ⏎, Queue ⇧⏎, Stop esc. The gate docks in the same column, so nothing overlaps. ThreadOne renderer. Runs and chat turns are the same block model with a mode flag.

Shell

  • Topbar keeps six things: mark, workspace switcher (name, branch, chevron), Chat | Code, engine dot, Ctrl+K, Settings. Dev-channel facts move to the Home footer and Settings.
  • Window frame. Frameless on Windows: the topbar is the drag region and carries the caption buttons, so there is one bar instead of a native title bar sitting over a toolbar.
  • Sidebar is one level deep: New session, search, an optional Needs-you group, then workspaces as headers with session rows (dot, title, time). Filter chips replace scrolling. Messages / Settings tabs go; Settings is in the topbar and Ctrl+,.
  • Changes dock on the right, 380 px, collapsible: Files with accept / reject per file, a hunk preview, Verify rows, Git state and "Review & commit…". It is the Plan → File changes → Verify → Git loop as a place, not four cards in the stream.
  • Home opens on what needs you, then recent workspaces and chat homes, with one field that opens a folder, jumps to a session or starts a prompt.

Trust surfaces

  • Gate above the composer: amber for shell and file, violet for a plan, cyan for a question. Every gate says what, where, why, and which policy is asking. "Allow npm for this session" is the trusted-class door, offered in the moment instead of in Settings.
  • Review is a full surface: file list, hunks with per-hunk accept, Verify results with the test list, a commit message Grok drafted and you own, and a footer that says exactly what accept and reject do to disk.
  • Receipts mark trusted runs ("trusted class · no prompt") and vendor tools Forge cannot show ("not available in Forge") in the same quiet voice, so the record is complete.
  • Line comments. Any diff line takes a comment that goes to Grok with the hunk as context (Ctrl+⏎), so review is a conversation, not only a verdict.
  • Chat keeps bubbles for you and prose for Grok; answers cite the pack page they came from, and long answers open in Beside instead of becoming a wall in the bubble.

5 · Canon: kept and proposed amendments

KeptVoidglass dark as default and brand reference · Geist + Geist Mono · cyan leads actions, violet is accent · glass over hard shadows · one gradient CTA per screen · amber dock for attention · activity groups, not a card per call · toasts bottom-right · no light default.
Proposed amendmentsRadius scale 16 / 12–14 / 10 · star field off by default (static aurora; stars under a preference) · receipts replace "Tool activity" · composer footer to one line and in flow · SVG mark in chrome · one Chip, one Button with sizes · retire the Aeon hardcodes.

6 · Build order

  • 1 · Tokens and chrome. Retire Aeon hardcodes; add surface and radius tokens; Button sizes; Chip; SVG mark; topbar and sidebar. Observable: no mint anywhere, sidebar one level deep.
  • 2 · Thread. Spine, You block, thought line, receipts, prose measure; delete "Answered" and "Your turn"; one renderer. Observable: a turn is at most four blocks.
  • 3 · Gate and Changes dock. Gate tiers with keys; dock with Files / Verify / Git; Review surface. Observable: approve, review and commit without leaving the column.
  • 4 · Composer and Home. One-row composer that grows, Plan / Expert / Review chips, context ring, Steer / Queue / Stop, anchored menus; frameless window; Home with Needs-you. Observable: the composer is one row idle and two rows typing, in every mode.
  • 5 · Chat and motion. Restyle homes, packs and the Beside panel on the new system; the twelve interactions; reduced-motion parity. Observable: a week of Chat without the operator, and nothing loops that isn't alive.
