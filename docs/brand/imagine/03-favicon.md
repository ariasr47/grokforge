**Use with:** `00-BRAND-BRIEF.md`  
**Aspect ratio:** **1:1**  
**Output:** large PNG master (will be scaled to 32×32)  
**Save as:** `forge-favicon-master.png`

## Brand context

Forge × Aeon Continuum. Favicon is the **simplest** expression of the nucleus — even bolder than the UI mark.
